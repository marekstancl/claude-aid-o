# English - website copy guide (/aid-ui)

AID's own text. Applies to pages with `lang="en"`. The adopted upstream files are already
English, so this guide points at them; `copy-rules.md` applies always.

## Kancelářština → běžné slovo

Use `adopted/marketingskills/copy-editing/references/plain-english-alternatives.md`
(complex word → plain alternative, phrases to remove).

## Prázdná slova

Use the vocabulary, puffery and hedge lists of
`adopted/marketingskills/copywriting/references/ai-tells.md`.

## Fráze prozrazující AI text

Use `adopted/marketingskills/copywriting/references/ai-tells.md` as written (bans and caps).

## Oslovení

Address the reader as "you"; the firm writes about itself in the first person, singular or
plural as it really is, and keeps it across the whole site.

## Typografie

Sentence case for headings, straight or curly quotes used consistently, no em dash in short
copy (as `ai-tells.md` says).

## Co zakázané NENÍ

- Claims are allowed only from `docs/content/facts.yaml` (`copy-rules.md`); everything else in
  the adopted files is guidance, not a ban beyond their own "Ban" marks.
- US-specific advice (free trial, demo booking) is used only when the customer really sells
  that way.
