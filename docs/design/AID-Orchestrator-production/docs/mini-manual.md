# Mini manual

## Clear space
Keep free space around the lockup at least equal to the height of the left input square.

## Minimum size
- Symbol: 16 px digital
- Full lockup: 120 px wide

## Correct use
- Use color-on-dark for dark developer interfaces.
- Use black on white backgrounds.
- Use white for strict monochrome dark applications.
- Use the supplied dark-background favicon as the default browser and PWA icon.

## Do not
- Do not alter the branch geometry.
- Do not rotate or stretch the mark.
- Do not replace the gradient.
- Do not add outlines, shadows, or effects.
- Do not recreate the wordmark with live text in deployed assets.
